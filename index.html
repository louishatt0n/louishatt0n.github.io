Personal Project — Bitcoin Backtester

Trading research in Python 2026

Two backtesting engines

A fast vectorised backtester and a day-by-day account simulator with commission and slippage, run side by side on momentum, moving-average and mean-reversion strategies.

Both engines agree on the ranking, and the results hold from zero to double the assumed costs.

Walk-forward out-of-sample test

Every six months each strategy re-tunes on the previous two years, then trades the next six months it has never seen: 2020 to 2026, after 0.15% costs per side, with the test plan committed before the first run.

Momentum matched holding Bitcoin's return with a smaller worst fall (−60% vs −77%) and a higher Sharpe (0.89 vs 0.59), though a bootstrap confidence interval shows the edge is not yet distinguishable from luck.

Testing the tuning itself

Compared each setting's rank in training with its rank out of sample.

Almost no link between the two: tuning mostly fitted noise, and the untuned 20/50 crossover beat the tuned one.
